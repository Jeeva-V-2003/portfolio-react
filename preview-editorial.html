About the work

I'm a full-stack developer with a particular interest in the craft of building — the architecture decisions that compound, the UI details that accumulate into a product that people actually enjoy using.

My background spans both sides of the stack. I'm as comfortable designing a database schema as I am refining a transition curve. I've shipped products used by tens of thousands of people, worked in fast-moving startups, and led engineering efforts from zero to production.

I believe in minimal dependencies, clear naming, and code that reads like prose. I'm drawn to projects where the problem is hard and the solution needs to be deceptively simple.
