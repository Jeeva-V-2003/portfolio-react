WHO I AM

Background · Philosophy · Approach

I'm Jeeva Vincent, a data engineer obsessed with building infrastructure that actually works at scale. Not just pipelines — entire data ecosystems that breathe, adapt, and deliver insight without drama.

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering precision and data architecture strategy. I don't just move data from A to B — I design the systems that make sure that data is clean, trusted, and fast when the business needs it.

From real-time Kafka streams to petabyte-scale Spark jobs to dbt transformation layers — I've built it, broken it, and rebuilt it better. Repeatability and observability are non-negotiable.

Based in the intersection of cloud infrastructure and modern data stack, I bring a product mindset to every pipeline: who uses this, what do they need, how do we make it impossible to get wrong?

"Data that arrives late is just noise. Data that arrives wrong is worse than nothing. I build systems where neither happens."
